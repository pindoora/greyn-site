GREYN

Film looks, drawn live.

GREYN is a photo and video editor for iPhone. Grain, halation, edge falloff and the paper border are computed on your device at full resolution — not stamped on from overlay images. What you see on screen is what lands in your library.

What it does

  • Film and analog colour, adjustable rather than fixed.
  • Story and collage templates that lay up to six photos out on one page.
  • Photo-strip layouts with real frame edges and sprocket holes.
  • Crop, straighten and quarter-turns that carry through to the export.
  • The same look applied to video, frame by frame.

What it does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No server. Your photos never leave the device.
  • No analytics, no advertising identifier, no tracking.

Coming to iOS

GREYN is built and on its way to the App Store. There is no date — Apple's review sets that, and a date promised twice is worse than no date at all.
